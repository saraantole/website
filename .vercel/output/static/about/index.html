What I Do

I'm a Senior AI Engineer at Consensys, where I build AI systems people use every day: retrieval-based search, agents, data pipelines, support automation end to end. I also built and ran the AI team inside our customer success department, which meant designing how a whole department would work with these tools, not just writing the code.

Out of that I built a framework for using AI well. I made it because I needed one, and it's what I work from now when I help businesses adopt AI in a way that fits the people doing the work. Most adoption starts with the tool and expects the humans to adjust around it. I'd rather start with the humans and choose the tools to fit.

Why

Technology should be at the service of a more human life. I believe that plainly, and I've spent enough time inside these systems to know it doesn't happen on its own.

In practice it comes down to three questions: which parts of your work you should actually hand over, how you keep the parts that are yours, and what you do with the time once you get it back. Answered well, AI gives you room. Answered badly, it gives you more of the same work, faster.

What I Love

Outside work I dance, I hike, I build things with my hands, and I spend as much time outdoors as I can get away with. None of it is productive, which is the point. It's also the honest answer to why I care about any of this: the time these tools give back has to go somewhere, and this is where mine goes.

— Sara
